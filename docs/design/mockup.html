PM (Enhanced)

Six live screens sharing one state, so a change in one phone shows up in the others. Times are Abu Dhabi sample data on the Asia/Dubai clock. Tap rows, switches, chips and pickers. Motion follows the dashboard system and turns off with your OS reduced-motion setting.

Tokens at a glance

Muted graphite neutrals with a slight green bias, one green in two strengths: a deep fill that carries white text, and a soft sage for text, icons and the countdown ring on dark surfaces. Ratios below are WCAG 2.x, computed live from the hex values. The full Compose mapping is in docs/design/design-tokens.md.
