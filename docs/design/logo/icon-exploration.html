PM (Enhanced) logo

New launcher icon for the app that silences the phone for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Jumu'ah, Taraweeh and Eid, then brings the sound back. Three directions were explored. The Mihrab Moon is the one shipped as Android vector drawables.

Three directions

Every preview uses the real adaptive-icon layers: a 108dp canvas with the launcher showing the middle 72dp, cut by each mask.

A

Hushed Wave

48px and 24px in circle, squircle and rounded-square masks

A crescent beside a sound wave that settles into a flat line: sound fading into the quiet of prayer.

Says "mute" the most directly, but the wave turns to noise at 24px and the shape looks like an audio app.

B

Mihrab Moon

Chosen

48px and 24px in circle, squircle and rounded-square masks

A pointed mihrab arch, open at the floor like a prayer niche, framing a crescent. The crescent is also the familiar "Do Not Disturb" moon.

Two shapes with one meaning: prayer time, phone asleep. Holds up at 24px, survives every mask and becomes a clean one-colour silhouette.

C

Prayer Clock

48px and 24px in circle, squircle and rounded-square masks

A dial ring that thins like a crescent, with two clock hands: the schedule that runs the app.

Good idea on paper, but at launcher size it reads as a generic clock or alarm app. Nothing in it says prayer.

Chosen: Mihrab Moon

The arch is a mihrab, the niche that shows the direction of prayer. It also carries over the arch drawn on the old prayer-mat icon, so existing users still recognise the app. The crescent marks the prayer hours and is the moon people already know as the Do Not Disturb symbol, which is exactly what the app turns on.

It is two solid shapes with no text, no figures and no sacred script. The frame is 5dp thick and the crescent is 22dp across on the 108dp canvas, so both still read as a silhouette at 24px and in the status bar.
